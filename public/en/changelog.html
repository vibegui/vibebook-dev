November 18, 2026

The new app is now the default

Opening vibebook now lands in the new app, on computers and phones. The old app is still there: turn off "New UI" in your account menu to go back to it on this device.

Back to the old app, per device

The "New UI" switch remembers your choice on this device (including the app installed on your phone): off, every page opens in the old app until you turn it back on, from the account menu on computers or the ☰ on phones.

Settings on one page

Clicking yourself at the foot of the sidebar opens the Settings page: profile, your agents, cloud agents and this device (notifications, theme, language, New UI, Face ID). The Freestyle key and the Claude token now live there, and "Cloud agent" in a room takes you straight to that section, on that room.

Improvements

  • People who already used vibebook see, once per device, a note about the new app with the way back to the previous version.
  • In your account menu, Notifications is a single switch: it turns sound and browser notifications on and off together, with no help dialog. New UI sits right below it.
  • On phones, your picture on the home screen moved to the right of the top bar, after search and +.
  • Opening a room with many archived epics is much lighter: archived epics arrive without their finished tasks (they load when you open the epic), and only the Claudes' status rows for the messages on screen come along.
  • The "Your agent is off in this room" notice left the top of the conversation: the delivery switch at the foot of the sidebar already shows it.
  • In the new app, "What needs you here" no longer shows up on its own: with the message box empty, the "What needs me here?" button asks your Claude, and it answers at the end of the thread in a message only you see.
  • Switching between direct messages is smooth now: the list stays put, the row you clicked doesn't move, and the new conversation fades in, without flashing a room's loading screen.
  • Switching between direct messages is smooth now: the list stays put, the row you clicked doesn't move, and the new conversation fades in, without flashing a room's loading screen.
  • Switching rooms is smooth too, from the room rail, the home or the browser's back button: no more loading flash, and the new conversation fades in.
  • Signing in with Google or GitHub now uses your picture from there as your profile picture, refreshed on each sign-in. If you upload a picture in vibebook, that one stays.
  • The computer's "online" dot at the foot of the sidebar is now lime, the same color as a listening agent.
  • Each person's picture (or generated face) is now the same everywhere: the direct messages list, your row at the bottom, the top of a direct conversation, messages, People, the board and the inbox. A direct conversation shows the other person's face and name at the top (while it loads too), in what's waiting on you and in the inbox, and a changed picture shows up everywhere right away, for the people in your rooms and conversations too.
  • In the English app, your conversation with yourself is called "You" at the top, on phones and in the tab's name (it used to show "Você"), and the @room suggestion, a quoted poll and the phone's agents tab follow the app's language too.
  • Using vibebook in English no longer shows stray Portuguese: error messages, phone notifications, the page that turns off mention emails, the terminal code page and the catch-up email now come in English.
  • Live site in a room: pin an https address as "live" (say a `cloudflared` tunnel to your localhost) and everyone sees the page inside vibebook, in a window marked as an outside site, with who pinned it, reload and open in a new tab. Room apps can show https pages inside them too.
  • In the sidebar, each of your Claudes shows the name of the room it listens to (the same as its herdr tab), with the folder on hover.
  • Apps embedded in a doc take the doc's whole width and "Open in the Apps panel" is now just the ↗ icon.
  • In the sidebar, each of your Claudes shows the name of the room it listens to (the same as its herdr tab), with the folder on hover.
  • A direct conversation opens on just the conversation: the side panel starts closed and the top bar no longer has the Doc button (Files, Apps, Decisions and Reminders are still there).
  • The sign-in screen now has the vibebook logo and the Google and GitHub icons on their buttons.
  • Empty Doc, Files, Apps, Decisions and Reminders tabs now look and read the same: what will show up there, a button to do it yourself, and a ready prompt for your Claude that lands in the message box in one click.
  • Files, Apps, Decisions and Reminders show a spinner in the middle of the panel while they load, instead of a written "Loading…".
  • Search (⌘K) now sits at the top of every room's sidebar too, as it already did in conversations.
  • Screens 641 to 900 px wide (tablets, a narrow window) now get the phone layout across the whole app, like the room already did. Before, the home page and some dialogs used the desktop one.

  • On phones, tapping the message box (or any text field) no longer zooms the screen in.
  • In a room with no Claude, "This computer" has a "Turn on here" button again (the old Turn on Claude): your vibebook herdr opens the room's Claude; with none running, the app offers the setup.

November 18, 2026

Nobody pulls you into a room or messages you without your yes

Room invites and messages from people you don't know now wait for you to accept, in one list, "Requests". Friend requests are gone: with anyone who shares a room with you, just talk.

Invites wait for your yes

Being invited to a room no longer puts you in it: the invite shows up in "Requests" (and in the email, which leads to the invite page) with Join or Decline. Until you join, nothing from the room reaches you. In the room, whoever invited sees the person under "Invited", as waiting or declined. Someone who asked to join through the link and was let in joins right away.

Message anyone

In the new app, the + on "Direct messages" opens "New message": anyone who shares a room with you opens the conversation right away. For any other email, you write the first message and it arrives as a request; the conversation opens only once they accept. Once declined it can't be sent again, and the answer never says whether the email has an account. The conversation list shows only people you've talked to, and Friends left the account panel and the menu.

The home starts with what waits on you, and your agent in one icon

In the new app (?ui=svelte), the home now starts with what waits on you, room by room, with the ask box docked at the bottom, on phones and computers. The room header shows your agent's state in a single icon.

Direct messages

In the new app, the logo on the room bar opens your direct messages: a private conversation with each friend, plus "You", just you and your Claude. People you've talked to come first; your other friends wait below for a first message. Each conversation has its own epics, which open under it. Friendship now needs both sides: adding someone sends a request, which shows at the top of their list to accept or decline.

Home by what waits on you

What waits on you comes first, grouped by room: rooms where your Claude is stuck waiting on you are on top, and each ask opens the message or resolves with ✓. Your rooms sit beside it (below, on phones), the "What do you want to work on?" box sits at the bottom like a room's message field, and search, + and the account menu live in the rooms bar, which on phones now stays put while you scroll. The install-the-app and add-your-photo prompts are now one slim row each.

Your machines and agents, with delivery per room

In the new app, the foot of the sidebar shows your computers (each running vibebook herdr) and your sandboxes, with each one's agents under it, and the agents you started by hand under "Other agents". Each computer has one switch per room: on delivers the room's messages to its agents (and opens one if there's none), off stops delivering without closing the tab, which keeps its context. Agents started by hand have their own switch, and the one on top turns everything on or off. A computer that stopped answering shows as Offline, with "copy fix". With nothing connected, three ways to start: this computer, a cloud sandbox, or just this once.

@room notifies everyone in the room

Write @room (or @sala, @todos) in a message to notify everyone in the room at once, and your Claude's short answer now stays in the question's thread.

@room

@room in your message mentions everyone in the room: each person gets the notification and each person's Claude sees it as addressed to their human. The @ autocomplete offers @room. Claudes' messages with @room don't notify anyone.

Improvements

  • In the new app (?ui=svelte), the epic list has a header: + opens the new-epic field in place and ✦ shows @vibe's suggestions, so the list only holds real epics.
  • On phones, the ☰ menu has its own "New UI" row to open the new app (?ui=svelte), instead of a cramped switch next to the theme.
  • In the new app (?ui=svelte), the room sidebar fits the screen again: the epic list, pins and people each scroll inside themselves instead of pushing the rest out of view.
  • In the new app (?ui=svelte), the Pinned and People & agents titles stay put while the list under them scrolls.
  • In the new app (?ui=svelte), the theme left the sidebar: it lives only in your account menu.
  • In the new app (?ui=svelte), a message's actions sit in one bar at its top-right corner, Discord-style: copy link, copy text, react (😊 opens the quick emojis) and reply, each with its tooltip.
  • In the new app (?ui=svelte), the message bar shows your 3 most used reactions and, after a divider, 😊 with the rest and the other actions, like Discord.
  • In the new app (?ui=svelte), button tooltips switch at once as the mouse moves from one button to the next, instead of flickering.
  • In the new app (?ui=svelte), the room header has a single right-panel button (⌥⌘B): "Open in the right panel", which did the same, is gone.

  • White initials on amber room covers had too little contrast; the color is now a bit darker.

  • In "Your connected agents", older CLI logins showed "usage unknown" while in use; they now show when they were last used.
  • In the new UI, pasting (⌘V) or dragging a file into the composer attached nothing; both attach again.
  • In the new UI, scrollbars were always showing; they are now thin and only show when you point at what scrolls, like the classic UI.

November 15, 2026

Docs with version history, and message links on the phone

The room doc and every epic doc now keep versions by themselves, and on the phone the copy gesture also copies a message's link.

Doc versions

When editing a doc goes quiet, vibebook saves a version, with nothing for you to do. At the top of the doc, pick a version to see how it was, restore it (the current one becomes a version too, nothing is lost) or jump to the chat at that moment.

Improvements

  • A question in the room opens its answer epic and doc by itself in under a second (@vibe sees it's a question), before your Claude even starts.
  • Dragging a message left shows two targets, Copy on top and Link below: with your finger on the message's top half it copies the text, on the bottom half it copies a link that opens the room right at it.
  • A question to your Claude opens the epic and its doc at once, with "Claude is writing the answer…" until it writes.
  • An app your Claude makes for an answer shows embedded in the doc (scrolled into view); the screen no longer jumps to the Apps tab by itself.
  • Emoji reactions on messages, in one bar under it: reactions on the left (🙂+ on hover, long press on the phone; chips show who reacted and a click undoes yours) and who saw it on the right. Claudes react too.
  • In an epic that answers a question, the task list sits at the end of the doc, under the answer.
  • "What needs you here" now opens with one or two written lines: what waits on you and why; the items fold below.
  • For a question, the Claude answers in one or two lines in the chat, and the doc beside it goes straight to the chart or table.
  • A question's epic is only the answer: no task list in the doc, no "What needs you here" and no @vibe notes.

Fixes

  • "Writing the answer…" showed in any epic's doc while a Claude worked; now only in question epics.
  • The doc version picker sits on the title line, at the right, and no longer closes by itself when you click it.
  • The list of who saw a message opens into the chat instead of hiding behind the doc.
November 14, 2026

Inbox, and what needs you in every epic

The inbox now gathers everything that needs you, across every room, in one place.

The answer shows up in the doc beside the chat

Ask something or ask for an analysis and your Claude starts an epic, opens its doc to your right and writes the answer there as it works: a native card (numbers, a table, a chart) when it's simple, an HTML page embedded in the doc when it's complex. Then keep talking in the chat and it improves the doc.

Cards that look like a dashboard

Cards posted by Claudes now draw charts with several series (legend, axis values and each series' number on hover), colored changes on numbers (green when it improves, red when it gets worse, including errors and cost, where down is good) with a small trend line under them, and real buttons.

What needs you here

At the end of every thread, a card only you see gathers what waits on you there: questions and asks for you, your Claude waiting on you, and your open tasks. ✓ clears one item; Dismiss hides the card until something new shows up.

Your Claude opens what it made beside the chat

Ask for a page (an HTML from a meeting, a report, a PDF): as soon as the first version is ready, your Claude opens it beside the chat, only in your tabs, and each new version shows up there by itself while you talk.

Talk instead of typing

Hold Space in the message box (or tap 🎤) and talk: the text shows up as you speak and, when you let go, is replaced by a better transcript made on the server with the room's names and epics. Enter sends it to your Claude like any message.

Apps inside the doc

Anywhere in a room's or an epic's doc, a room app or an MCP app runs live in the middle of the text. With the app open in the Apps tab, Put in doc adds it at the end; P, M and G on the card change its height. Claudes add one with an [[app:...]] line too.

Improvements

  • The inbox now shows, besides what's waiting on you, the mentions of you and your open tasks (to do, doing, review) across every room — in three sections, with a this-room / all toggle
  • The inbox button also sits in the room sidebar, right above the epic list
  • The open epic stands out in the list, with a tint and a color bar, not only bold
  • An archived epic you open shows in the tree, in its place, and leaves when you switch threads
  • Opening a room sends less data: only your messages your Claude hasn't closed yet
  • Inside a room, the inbox opens like an epic: in the middle, what was said to you (asks, your Claude waiting, mentions, @vibe's notes); on the right, a doc with what waits on your OK and your tasks by epic
  • The router is now on for everyone: your messages wake your Claude unless Jev clearly sees you're talking to someone else (so you can talk without your agent answering); other people's that aren't for you are left out without a model call; Jev weighs the rest, including whether a message tries to get access or secrets out of your Claude
  • An HTML file open beside the chat isn't shown twice: in the chat it becomes a small row ("open beside"), and the preview comes back when you close the panel
  • On an HTML card, Open opens it beside the chat; full screen is now the small icon on the right
  • The router learns from you: when you tell your Claude "that wasn't for you" (or that it missed a message), it becomes a test case every change to the router must pass
  • In Fire mode, when a native card can answer (metrics, a dashboard, a table, a list), Haiku builds the card in the chat and each new piece of speech updates the same card; an HTML page only when it needs one
  • 🎤: one tap starts dictation and another stops it (no holding); holding for 3 seconds turns on Fire mode
  • Fire mode: the 🎤 sits between the text box and Send; each spoken piece goes after 0.7 s of no change (it used to wait for the browser to close the sentence), with a loading sign while you're still talking; holding the 🎤 on a phone no longer selects text; in vibebook's metrics rooms the card uses yesterday's numbers
  • Hybrid Fire mode: the fast model draws right away and each spoken piece also wakes your Claude, which checks it with real data and fixes the card (fire_card tool) or the page while you talk
  • Fire mode switches format mid-session: "make an html" leaves the card for a page, "make a card" goes back; asking about infrastructure and spend uses the product and AI-cost numbers
  • A message sent while a page is open beside the chat carries which page you were looking at, so "change this" reaches your Claude with the right file
  • Apps inside the doc take the doc's full width
  • An epic that shipped, but where you asked for changes afterwards, shows "🚀↻ changes in progress" instead of just "live"
  • New page for MCP server owners: how vibebook says who is calling (X-Vibebook-Identity), how to verify it safely, and a ready prompt for your Claude (/en/mcp-identity)
  • On phones, sliding 🎤 up locks Fire mode: it keeps listening hands-free, sends each piece at a pause, and stops only when you tap 🎤 again

Fixes

  • A spinner on 🎤 shows while Whisper improves the dictated text.
  • Enter while dictating waits for the better transcript (Whisper) and sends it, not the browser's draft.
  • On phones, a drag from the left edge goes back (epic to room, room to home, in the installed app) instead of replying to the message
  • Your Claude's queue no longer lists your "Aprovar"/"Negar" answers to its asks as requests
  • Two Claude Code sessions of yours in one folder are now two agents in the room, each with its own reads, and People warns when more than one of yours listens to the same room; a listen from an old CLI exits by itself when the CLI is updated
  • The room's ↗ opens the room's Claude in herdr through vibebook (server → your CLI), without asking the browser for local network permission
  • Typing in the message box is lighter: the box only resizes when its line count changes, and on phones the space no longer goes through the dictation shortcut (which froze the iPhone keyboard)
  • Link buttons in cards showed as underlined links
  • Sending while dictating now stops the 🎤 and no longer writes the sent text back into the box
  • The "2 of your Claudes are listening to this room" warning no longer counts the Fire mode Haiku, which is a second Claude on purpose
  • An HTML your Claude uploads opens beside the chat by itself (and a room app opens in Apps), no click needed; a room app shows in the chat as one row instead of being drawn twice
  • In Fire mode the Haiku gets the rule (numbers and charts = card) and your words first, the room context last: it used to cut the output and miss the rule
  • Leaving Fire, your main Claude no longer redoes what the Haiku already answered
  • Turning Fire off no longer closes the Haiku mid-answer: it finishes, and if Fire comes back within a minute the same tab is reused

November 10, 2026

Apps in the room, MCP connections

The Apps tab brings together everything that runs inside a room: MCP servers you connect and apps your Claude builds for it.

Connect MCP servers to a room

Paste an MCP server URL (and a token if it needs one) and choose: for the room, so everyone can use it, or just for you. The token is stored encrypted and never reaches the browser or the Claudes. If the server has apps, they open in the panel next to the chat.

Ask your Claude to connect it
